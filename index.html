About me

A bit about me.

I studied geology in Colombia, then remote sensing at the University of Twente/ITC. Now, at KU Leuven, I bring together petrography, mineralogy, spectral and geochemical imaging, Python, GIS and machine learning to study LCT pegmatites. I like sharing the science in a clear, approachable way.
